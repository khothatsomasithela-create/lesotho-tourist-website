About Lesotho

Lesotho is an independent, high-altitude nation in Southern Africa completely surrounded by South Africa, known as the "Kingdom in the Sky". Entirely above 1,000 metres in elevation, it features the Maloti and Drakensberg mountain ranges and the highest low point of any country in the world (1,400 metres). Maseru is the capital and largest city. Home to roughly 2.3 million people, primarily of the Basotho ethnic group. The official languages are Sesotho and English. The Lesotho loti (LSL) is pegged at parity with the South African Rand (ZAR); both currencies are widely accepted.

Some of the destinations in Lesotho

Waterfalls Nature

Maletsunyane Falls is a 192-metre (630 ft) single-drop horsetail waterfall near the town of Semonkong in the Maseru District.

Human made (Dams) Nature

The Katse Dam is a concrete double-curvature arch dam, 185 metres (607 ft) high, on the Malibamatso River in the Thaba-Tseka District.

Natural / Mountains Nature

Sehlabathebe National Park is Lesotho's oldest nature reserve and part of the Maloti-Drakensberg World Heritage Site, in the remote south-east of the Qacha's Nek District.

Cultural and Arts Culture

The Kome Caves are cave dwellings made of mud in the Berea District, 25 km east of Teyateyaneng. One of the caves is still inhabited by descendants of the original settlers.

Adventure / Snow sports Adventure

Afriski is the only ski resort in Lesotho, located 3,050 m above sea level in the Maluti Mountains near the northern border with South Africa.

Historical Culture

Thaba Bosiu is a historic sandstone plateau and the spiritual birthplace of the Basotho nation, 24 km east of Maseru.
